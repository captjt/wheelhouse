# Security policy

Wheelhouse runs on your Mac, as you, beside agents that also run as you.
[docs/security.md](docs/security.md) says what it protects and what it
doesn't.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please don't open a public issue. Report it privately through GitHub's
[security advisories](https://github.com/captjt/wheelhouse/security/advisories/new)
for this repo, with what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it lets
someone do.

You'll get an answer within a week. Fixes land on `main`; there are no
released versions to backport to yet.
