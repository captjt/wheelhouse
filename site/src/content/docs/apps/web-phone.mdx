The hub serves the web app itself. It's the same Wheelhouse as
Wheelhouse.app: the same lists, dockets, Chat, Reviews, Builds, Recap and
Settings, on the same record.

## In a browser

Open [http://127.0.0.1:7500](http://127.0.0.1:7500) on the Mac the hub runs
on. `wh open` opens it there too, or opens Wheelhouse.app when you have it.

It listens on this Mac only (127.0.0.1), and answers only requests that name
one of its own hosts. `WH_HTTP` moves it, or `off` turns it off: see
[Configuration](/reference/configuration/).

## On your phone

Your phone reaches the app over [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com), on your
own tailnet, answering only your Tailscale user.

<Steps>

1. Install Tailscale on the Mac and the phone, sign in to both with the same
   account, and turn on HTTPS for your tailnet in Tailscale's admin console.

2. On the Mac, publish the app:

   ```sh
   wh web --publish
   ```

   It's served through `tailscale serve` at
   `https://<your-mac>.ts.net:7500`. `wh web` prints the address, and the user
   it answers.

3. Open that address in Safari on the phone, then **Share → Add to Home
   Screen**.

4. Open Wheelhouse from the Home Screen and allow its notifications. That's
   Web Push: prompts, questions and finished turns reach your phone like any
   app's.

</Steps>

Anyone else on your tailnet gets a 403. Every request must carry your
Tailscale login.

<Aside type="tip">
`wh doctor` says whether Tailscale is seen and the app is published, and what
to do about each.
</Aside>

## Laid out for a phone

On a phone the app is laid out natively, not as a shrunken desktop:

- **A tab bar** with the Inbox, Active and the rest, and large titles.
- **A docket slides onto its list.** Swipe right to go back.
- **Rows that swipe:** Done, Drop and Status, or Approve, Deny and File in
  the Inbox. Long-press a row for its menu.
- **Sheets from the bottom,** and pull to refresh.
- **Chat first.** An agent's docket opens to its conversation: what you
  asked, what it answered, what it's doing now, and a box to send the next
  thing. What you send while it works is queued; its reply comes back as a
  push.
- **Kept files open here,** pinned by their entry's hash, and so do previews
  an agent shares with `wh share`.

## Light, dark, or the system's

The theme follows your system. To pin Light or Dark, use the switch at the
bottom of the sidebar. Wheelhouse.app has the same choice in Settings →
General.

## Keys

The web app has the same keys as Wheelhouse.app: ⌘K or `/` to search, `C` for
a new docket, `G` then a letter to go. See
[Keyboard shortcuts](/apps/shortcuts/).
