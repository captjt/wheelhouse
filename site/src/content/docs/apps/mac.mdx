On the Mac, Wheelhouse is a native app, written in SwiftUI. It has the web
app's look and words in the system's own controls. It follows the hub on this
Mac, so everything you do in it is on the same record as the web app, the
phone and `wh`.

`just setup` puts it in `~/Applications`. See [Install](/start/install/).

<Shot name="mac-docket" alt="Wheelhouse.app: the sidebar, the Active list grouped by what needs you, and a docket beside it with its intent's acceptance boxes, the two files it kept, its signed activity, and its properties in the inspector" />

## The window

- **A sidebar** with your places: Inbox, Reviews, Builds, Active, All, Done,
  Agents and Recap, then your Rex and Herdr sessions. ⌘1 to ⌘8 go to each.
- **Each list beside its docket,** as Mail does. Pick a row and its docket
  opens to the right. The docket's properties (status, repo, links, its
  agents and their panes) are in an inspector.
- **A docket** shows its intent with its acceptance boxes, the files it kept,
  its Chat with the agent, and its signed activity. Tick a box, chat, or read
  the record without leaving the window.
- **⌘K** searches dockets and commands.
- **A window of its own.** Double-click a docket, or press ↩ on its row, to
  open it in a separate window.
- **All dockets as a board,** grouped by status. Drag a card to change its
  status.

## ⌥⌘K from any app

Wheelhouse's palette floats over whatever app you're in. What needs you comes
first: approve or deny a prompt, pick one of an agent's answers, post a review.
Below that, find a docket, create one, or start an agent. You never switch
apps.

<Shot name="mac-quick" size="narrow" alt="⌥⌘K over any app: what needs you first (approve or deny a prompt, an agent's two answers), then Create and your dockets" />

Turn it off in Settings → General if the chord is taken.

## The menu bar

The wheel sits in the menu bar. It turns orange with a count when something
needs you. Its panel answers in place: Approve, Deny, an agent's options, Post.

## Notifications

Allow Wheelhouse's notifications the first time you open it. Each notification
carries its answer:

- a permission prompt: **Approve** or **Deny**
- a question: reply, or pick one of its options
- a review ready to post: **Post** or **Dismiss**
- a finished turn from an agent you messaged

Each one is withdrawn once it's answered, here or on your phone. The banner's
buttons work only on an unlocked Mac.

They sound like the Mac, or like a [peon-ping](https://www.peonping.com/) pack
you pick in Settings → Notifications: an orc peon's "Work complete!" when an
agent finishes. **Send a Test** tries it.

While Wheelhouse.app is open, it posts the Mac's notifications. The hub posts
none of its own.

## The Mac's own ways

- **Quick Look.** A kept file opens in Quick Look once its bytes match the
  hash on the record.
- **Drop a file on a docket** to keep it there, pinned by its hash.
- **Swipe a row** on the trackpad: Done, Drop or Status in a list; Approve,
  Deny or File in the Inbox.
- **The Dock.** Its badge counts what needs you, and its menu lists it.
- **The menus** have every command, with its keys. See
  [Keyboard shortcuts](/apps/shortcuts/).

## The Docket menu

These act on the docket in front of you, in the main window or its own.

| Command | Keys | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open in Rex, or Open in Herdr | ⇧⌘O | Takes you to the agent's pane |
| Peek at Pane | ⇧⌘P | Shows the pane's screen without leaving Wheelhouse |
| Read the Record | ⇧⌘R | Every entry, who signed it, and how it's known |
| View Changes | ⇧⌘D | The docket's code diff against its base, or between rounds |
| Status | ⌥⌘1 to ⌥⌘4 | To do, In progress, Done, Dropped |
| Run Checks | ⇧⌘U | Its repo's witnessed checks, now |
| Hold, or Resume | ⇧⌘H | Pause its protocol, with a reason on the record |
| Start an Agent, or Add an Agent | ⇧⌘A | An agent on this docket, beside any already there |
| Link a Linear Issue… | | Tie an issue to it |
| Verify Every Record | | Check every docket's record, kept files included |

## Settings

⌘, opens Settings. The Mac app's own preferences are in **General**,
**Notifications** and **Hub**. The rest (standing orders, runtimes, agents,
repos, guides and briefs) are the same as the web app's: see
[Settings](/reference/settings/).

| Pane | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| General | Appearance (System, Light or Dark), the menu bar icon, ⌥⌘K, open at login |
| Notifications | Notify me about what needs me, macOS's permission, a test, the sound pack |
| Hub | The hub's address (`http://127.0.0.1:7500`, unless you run a second one), its status, **Start the Hub**, **Show the Log**, **Verify Every Record** |

<Aside>
If the app says it can't reach the hub, run `wh status` in a terminal, or
press **Start the Hub**. `wh up` runs it at login. See [the wh CLI](/reference/cli/#the-hub).
</Aside>
