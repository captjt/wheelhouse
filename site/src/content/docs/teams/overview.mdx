A **workspace** joins your Wheelhouse with your teammates': a wheelhouse of
wheelhouses. You share the dockets you choose. Everyone sees who's on each one,
people and agents alike. Your agents work with theirs across Macs, on one
signed record that every hub checks.

Your own hub stays yours. Your panes and what's on their screens, your
credentials and your keys never leave your Mac, your approvals leave only
redacted (the team sees that you approved, not what), and nobody
approves, starts or drives anything on your hub but you.

## How it fits together

- **A workspace hub orders.** It's the same `wh`, run as
  `wh serve --workspace` on a machine that stays on: a Linux box, a VM, or an
  always-on Mac on your tailnet. It gives each entry on a shared docket its
  place in line, and checks it against the rules first. It signs nothing
  anyone relies on: every entry is signed by the person, hub or agent that
  wrote it.
- **Each member's hub keeps a checked copy** of who's in the workspace and of
  every shared docket. It takes an entry only if it follows the chain it
  already has, is new, and passes the rules. So a workspace hub that changed,
  dropped or reordered an entry, or slipped in a key nobody issued, is caught,
  and your hub says so.
- **The tailnet gets you in; keys prove who wrote what.** The workspace hub
  answers only requests that came through `tailscale serve` with a member's
  Tailscale login, signed with that member's key. It works with one shared
  tailnet, or with the workspace's machine shared into each teammate's own.

## Your workspace in the apps

Once you've joined, the workspace has a place in the sidebar under its name,
in the web app and in Wheelhouse.app, and on the phone under **More**. It
shows:

- **Whether you're connected.** Your writes wait in an outbox while you're
  not, and go when the workspace hub is back. The sync button sends them now,
  and says why not if it can't.
- **Held back:** anything you wrote that looks like a secret (a GitHub token,
  an AWS key, a private key) stays on your Mac until you say **Send anyway**
  or **Drop**. Something the workspace refused shows here too.
- **Shared dockets**, each with its status, the agents on it and whose they
  are, and its people.
- **Members**, with their role and Tailscale login.

## Shared dockets

Share one of yours, or open one straight on the workspace:

```sh
wh team share D-12              # D-12 goes on as ACME-n
wh new --team "Rate limits"     # a new one, shared from the start
```

A share takes the docket's title, status, intent and protocol as they are.
Its history stays on your Mac, since it holds your approvals and prompts
unredacted. The share names that history by its head, so you can show it
later, checkably. `D-12` stays a name for `ACME-n` on your hub.

A shared docket has any number of **owners** and **members**. The person who
shared it is its first owner.

| | Owners (and their agents) | Members (and their agents) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notes, files, messages, decisions | Yes | Yes |
| Agents on it, prompts and turns | Yes | Yes |
| Change its intent or status | Yes | No |
| Add or remove people, make owners | Yes | Add themselves |
| Answer its agents' questions | Yes | When the owners say so |

```sh
wh team people ACME-1 sam --owner      # make Sam an owner
wh team people ACME-1 dana             # add Dana as a member
wh team people ACME-1 --answer members # let members answer questions too
```

Its page shows its owners and members, every teammate's agent on it, and where
it came from. Its activity names each teammate's entries as theirs ("sam
noted", "sam's Codex wrote to @ACME-1"), and keeps "You" for yours. The record
checks the same way a docket of your own does: **Verified** opens it, with who
signed every entry.

## Agents across Macs

Start an agent on a shared docket from your own Mac (`wh start --docket
ACME-1`, or **Add an agent** on its page); your teammates do the same from
theirs. Every agent's `wh brief` lists the others, whoever's Mac they're on.

- **Messages.** `wh send --to @ACME-1 "…"` from an agent reaches every agent
  on the docket, teammates' too. The message travels as an entry on the
  docket, so it's on the record like everything else.
- **A teammate's agents wait for you.** A message from a teammate's agents
  lands in your Inbox, not your pane, and waits until you choose: **Deliver**
  it, **Drop** it, or **Let Sam's agents through** on that docket, after which
  theirs go straight in. When one is delivered, your agent sees it marked as a teammate's agent's
  request, never as yours. `wh team trust ACME-1 sam --off` asks you again.
- **Questions.** An agent's question (`wh send --to me`) on a shared docket
  goes to its own person and to every owner. Whoever answers first, that
  answer stands everywhere, and every hub takes down its own copy of the
  question. Permission prompts go only to the agent's own person.
- **Each hub minds its own agents.** Your hub follows, releases and messages
  only the agents on your Mac. It never types into a teammate's pane, and
  nothing from a teammate's hub can approve, start, stop or hold anything on
  yours.

## What travels, and what stays

| Goes to the workspace | Goes, redacted | Never leaves your Mac |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intent, status, notes, files you keep with it, messages, questions and answers, checks, which agents are on it, your prompts and your agents' turns | Approvals and blocked prompts: who approved, in whose agent, and a digest of what. The full text stays on your Mac. | Pane screens, permission prompt text, credentials, keys, and every docket you haven't shared |

- **Private prompts.** `wh team trust ACME-1 --private-prompts on` keeps your
  prompts on that docket to yourself: the team sees that you prompted an
  agent, and a digest, the way it sees an approval.
- **Secrets are held.** Before anything leaves, your hub checks it for what
  looks like a secret. One that does waits under **Held back**, and in
  Attention, until you send it anyway or drop it (`wh team send N`,
  `wh team drop N`).
- **Pushing, posting, opening a PR** only ever run from the hub of the person
  who approved them.

## On your phone

The workspace and its shared dockets are on your phone too, through your own
hub as everything else is: the workspace under **More**, a shared docket's
people under its **ⓘ**, and a teammate's agent's message in the Inbox, to let
in from wherever you are.

## What you're trusting

Every entry is signed by whoever wrote it, and every member's hub checks the
chain. So the workspace hub is trusted to stay up and to order entries, not to
tell the truth about them.

| Who | Can | Can't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A teammate's agent | Write on shared dockets as its own member's agent; message your agents, which waits for you unless you trusted them | Type into your panes unasked, approve, start, stop, push or post on your hub, or write as anyone else |
| A member's hub, compromised | Write anything its member could, on dockets it can see | Write as another member, take an invite meant for someone else, or rewrite what's been ordered |
| The workspace hub, misbehaving | Withhold entries, refuse writes, see everything shared | Forge an entry, add a key or a member without an admin's signature, or rewrite, drop or reorder what a hub already has without that hub noticing |
| A removed member | Keep what they had already copied | Write anything new: their keys are refused from the removal on |
| Anyone else on the tailnet | Reach the workspace hub's hello | Anything else: no member login, no member key |

<Aside type="caution" title="What it doesn't stop yet">
A workspace hub can show a member a docket that stops early: withholding looks
the same as quiet. And two members who never compare heads could be shown
different futures. Cosigned checkpoints are planned to close both. What's
shared is also readable by whoever runs the workspace hub's machine, so choose
its operators as you'd choose admins.
</Aside>

## When something's off

- **Not connected.** The workspace hub is down or out of reach. Nothing is
  lost: your writes wait in the outbox. `wh team sync` says why.
- **"Not followed" on a shared docket.** Your hub refused something the
  workspace hub served: a changed entry, a fork, or a key no member issued. It
  took none of it in, and says what in Attention and in `wh verify ACME-1`.
  Tell the workspace's admin; `wh team follow ACME-1` tries again from where
  it stopped.
- **Removed from the workspace.** What you wrote before still checks, and you
  keep your copy. Your keys are refused from the removal on.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `wh team` | The workspace: connected or not, its members, its shared dockets, anything held |
| `wh team create NAME --prefix ACME --url URL --setup CODE` | Make a workspace on a workspace hub, as its first admin |
| `wh team invite HANDLE [--admin] [--hours N]` | An invite code, for one person, once |
| `wh team join CODE [--login L] [--name N]` | Join with an invite code |
| `wh team members` | Who's in, with their role and login |
| `wh team share D-12`, `wh new --team "title"` | Share a docket of yours, or open one shared |
| `wh team people ACME-1 [HANDLE] [--owner\|--remove] [--answer owners\|members]` | Who's on a shared docket, and who answers its agents' questions |
| `wh team trust ACME-1 [HANDLE] [--off] [--private-prompts on\|off]` | Let a teammate's agents' messages straight in; keep your prompts to yourself |
| `wh team role HANDLE admin\|member`, `wh team remove HANDLE`, `wh team leave` | Admins: roles and removals; anyone: leave |
| `wh team send N`, `wh team drop N` | Send or drop an entry held back |
| `wh team sync`, `wh team follow ACME-1` | Sync now; follow a docket your hub stopped following |
| `wh verify ACME-1`, `wh verify ACME-0` | Check your copy of a shared docket, or of the member log |
| `wh serve --workspace`, `wh up --workspace` | Run a workspace hub, or keep one running on an always-on Mac |
