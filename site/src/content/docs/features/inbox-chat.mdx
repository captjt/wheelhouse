Agents ask only what only you can decide. The **Inbox** is where those asks
land, from every agent, in Rex and Herdr alike. **Chat** is how you talk back:
to any agent, from the Mac or your phone, while it works or between turns.

## The Inbox

The Inbox is your open asks, ordered by the cost of waiting. An agent that has
stopped for you comes first.

| What | Why it's there | You |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A permission prompt** | An agent wants to run something and is stopped until you answer. | **Approve** or **Deny** |
| **A review ready to post** | A Review round's checked review is waiting for you, with its verdict, blocking findings and merge risk. | **Post**, **Preview** or **Dismiss** |
| **A message** | An agent (or Build) asked a question. Its answers come as buttons, its recommendation first. | Pick one, or answer in your own words |
| **A failure** | An agent stopped with an error. | Open it, or mark the docket done |
| **A finished turn** | An agent finished and is waiting for your next word. Its last message is the line you see. | Reply, or mark it done |

On a docket with several agents, each agent that's asking is its own item, by
name.

Below your asks, **Attention** lists what's worth a look but isn't an ask: a
protocol that stopped with an error or a stall, a required check failing on an
open docket, a docket on hold, an agent that's worked over an hour without
finishing a turn, a docket that's gone quiet for a week, and one you closed on
your word. [Verify](/features/verify/#attention) has the full list.
`wh attention` prints it in a terminal.

### Answer from anywhere

The same asks reach you wherever you are, and an answer in one place clears it
everywhere:

- **The window.** The Inbox, or the ask's card on its docket.
- **The menu bar.** The wheel turns orange with a count, and its panel answers
  in place: Approve, Deny, an agent's options, Post.
- **A notification.** Approve a prompt, reply to an agent, pick an option or
  post a review from the banner. It's withdrawn once it's answered anywhere.
- **⌥⌘K from any app.** What needs you comes first in the floating palette.
- **Your phone.** The Inbox from your Home Screen, with Web Push. Swipe a
  prompt to approve or deny it, a message to file it, a finished turn to
  close its docket.
- **The terminal.** `wh` lists what needs you; `wh reply 3 --choice "…"`
  answers a message.

Before a key goes into a pane, the hub reads the prompt again from its own
runtime. If the agent has moved on, or it's now a different prompt, your old
answer goes nowhere.

<Aside type="note">
Claude Code's permission prompts are answered from the apps today. Codex's and
Antigravity's prompts show in the Inbox; to answer one, **Open in Rex** or
**Open in Herdr** takes you to its pane.
</Aside>

### Keys

In the Inbox, `J` and `K` move, `↩` opens, `A` and `D` approve or deny a prompt,
`1` to `6` pick a message's option, and `E` marks a docket done.
[Keyboard shortcuts](/apps/shortcuts/) has the rest.

## Chat

An agent's docket opens to its conversation: what you asked, what it answered
(rendered as it wrote it, tables and all), the questions it asked you and what
you chose, the files it kept, and what it's doing now. At the bottom is a box to
send it the next thing.

### Sending while it works

- **Idle,** what you send goes straight into its pane.
- **Working,** what you send is **queued**. Everything queued goes in together
  when its turn ends. Until then, each queued message shows under the
  conversation with **Take back**.
- **Send now** (⌘⇧↩) steers the running turn instead: the agent reads it at its
  next step, without waiting for the turn to end. Claude Code and Codex take
  it.

`↩` sends (or queues), and `⇧↩` is a new line. On a phone, the buttons send.

When an agent replies to something you sent from Chat, the reply comes to your
phone as a push, so you can put it down and walk away.

### Several agents on one docket

When a docket has more than one agent, Chat says which one wrote each message,
and a menu beside the box picks which agent you're sending to, with its runtime.
See [Agents on a docket](/concepts/agents/).

### From the docket's Activity

Chat talks to one agent. The composer at the foot of a docket's Activity talks
to the docket: `@tests …` or `@all …` instructs its agents, `/ask` puts a
question to them with its answers as buttons, `/decide` records a decision
they'll all work from, and `/task` adds another agent. See
[the composer](/concepts/dockets/#the-composer).

### From an agent

Agents reach you too. From its pane, an agent can `wh send --to me` with its
answers as `--option` buttons, its recommendation first, and say what it will
do meanwhile. That's a message in your Inbox; your answer lands in its prompt as
`[wheelhouse] Message #N`, with `Chose:` when you pressed a button.
