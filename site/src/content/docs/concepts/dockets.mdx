A **docket** is one piece of work, from the first ask to the last merge. It's
numbered `D-41`, and a docket shared with your team is numbered by its
workspace, like `ACME-12`. Every agent is on a docket, and everything that
happens to a docket is an entry on its [record](/concepts/record/).

<Shot name="mac-docket" alt="Wheelhouse.app: the Active list grouped by what needs you, and a docket beside it with its intent's acceptance boxes, the files it kept, its signed activity, and its properties in the inspector" />

## How a docket opens

- **You start an agent.** Type `claude`, `codex`, `agy` or `pi` in a Rex pane or
  a Herdr terminal, and a docket appears. Claude Code's and Antigravity's hooks
  and Pi's extension name it from your first prompt and add your prompts and
  their turns; a Codex is followed by its pane only, so its docket keeps the
  pane's title (unless `wh start --codex` started it, with its prompt). `/clear`
  in Claude Code (or `/new` in Pi) starts a new docket for the same pane.
- **Wheelhouse starts one.** `wh start "Fix the flaky retry test"` opens a pane
  with Claude Code (or `--codex`, `--agy`, `--pi`) in a folder (`-C DIR`), on a
  new docket, with your prompt.
- **By hand.** `wh new "Paginate exports"`, or **New docket** (`C`) in the app.
  `wh new --linear ENG-142` opens one from a Linear issue.
- **A protocol opens it.** A PR that requests your review becomes a Review
  docket. A Linear issue with a label you chose can start a Build.

## What a docket holds

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Title and status** | Status is To do, In progress, Done or Dropped. An agent's docket starts In progress. |
| **Intent** | The docket's living spec: what must be true when it's done, acceptance boxes, and what's out of scope. Agents read it in their brief and keep it current. |
| **Activity** | Every entry, in order: your prompts, each turn's last message, notes, questions and answers, approvals, checks, and files. Each says how it's known. The [composer](#the-composer) is at its foot. |
| **Decisions** | What's been decided on it, yours and the agents' answered questions. The newest for a subject stands, and every agent's brief carries it. |
| **Agents** | Every agent on it, in Rex or Herdr, each by its pane, with **Open**, its screen, and a way to message it. |
| **Files** | What the work made and you'll want later: a plan, a report, a screenshot, a link to a Claude artifact. Each is pinned by its hash. |
| **Links** | The Linear issue or GitHub PR it came from, read again from the source. |
| **Changes** | For a docket with code on this Mac: the files its agent touched and their diff, from the base or any point on its record, with your [comments on its lines](#changes). |
| **Verify** | Its checks, their results at each commit, and whether its record is intact. |

## The intent

The intent says what done means. Write it in the app, or with `wh intent`:

```sh
wh intent D-12                     # print it
wh intent D-12 - < intent.md       # set it from a file
```

A task list in the intent becomes the docket's **acceptance boxes**. Tick them
in the app as the work lands. Agents follow the intent, and update it when the
goal or scope really changes, so it stays true. A docket with an intent can
become a [Build](/features/build/): **Build it** turns it into a plan, then a PR.

## The composer

At the foot of a docket's Activity, in Wheelhouse.app and the web app, is one
box. What you type there is a note, unless it starts with a command:

| Type | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| `Retries look right to me` | A note on the record, signed as you. Markdown works. |
| `@tests fix the flaky retry test` | Instructs that agent on the docket, by its pane's name, its role or its app, as a prompt. `@all` instructs every agent on it. While an agent works, it waits and goes in when the turn ends. |
| `/ask Which store? \| Postgres \| SQLite` | Asks the docket's agents, with the options after each `\|` as buttons. The answer stands under **Decisions**. |
| `/decide Retries: 5xx and timeouts only` | Records a decision of yours, as `Subject: answer`, and tells the agents on the docket. It stands under **Decisions** in every `wh brief`. A later one for the same subject replaces it. |
| `/decide #12 Postgres` | Answers a question that waits on you, by its key (`#12`, or a Build's `D1`, `D2`), or by the question's own words before the colon. When you asked several agents, the others hear the answer too. |
| `/task write the migration's tests` | Starts another agent on the docket: **Add an agent…** opens with its prompt filled in (`Read wh brief D-12, then: write the migration's tests`), for you to pick the agent and where it runs. |
| `/note /tmp is full again` | A note that starts with `/` or `@`. |

`/` and `@` complete as you type: the commands, and the agents on the docket by
name. The button says what will happen (**Note**, **Send**, **Ask**, **Decide**,
**Add an agent…**) before you press it. A path like `/tmp/x` on its own is a
note, not a command.

<Aside type="tip">
A decision stands for its docket. To make it stand for the whole repo, press
**Make standing for acme/api** beside it under **Decisions**: its rule goes in
the repo's notes, so every later brief there carries it.
</Aside>

## Changes

A docket with code on this Mac shows what it changed: **+N −M** on its page,
and the whole diff in **Changes** (`D` on the docket in the web app, ⇧⌘D in
Wheelhouse.app). Compare from the base, or any point on its record such as a
round's head or a checked commit, to HEAD, a later point, or what isn't
committed yet. It's split or unified, highlighted, with the files listed beside
it.

### Comments on a line

Hover a line and click the **+** in its gutter, or drag it across several
lines. A box opens under them. Write what should change (Markdown), and
**Comment** (⌘↩).

- It goes on the record as a note of yours, with what it's on: the file, the
  side (the new lines or the old), the lines, the commits compared, and the
  code it was on. It reads on its own after the lines move.
- The agents on the docket are told, unless you untick **Tell**: a message
  with the place, the code and your words, between their turns.
- It shows under its lines on the version it was written on. On any other,
  it's listed above the diff with **Show**, which compares what it was written
  on. One on uncommitted changes says **changed since** once the agent has
  changed those lines.
- Each file in the list counts its comments, and the Activity shows each one
  with its code.

## Files a docket keeps

An agent keeps a file with `wh attach report.md --title "Load test results"`,
or a link with `wh attach https://…`. You can do the same from a shell with
`--docket D-12`, or drop a file on a docket in Wheelhouse.app. A file is kept
once, by its sha256, and the record pins those exact bytes. It stays with the
docket after the scratch folder it came from is gone. A file is at most 25 MB.

Kept files open in the app and on your phone. On the Mac they open in Quick
Look, once their bytes match the record's hash.

<Aside type="caution">
Don't attach secrets, `.env` files, or anything with credentials in it. On a
shared docket, what you keep goes to the whole team.
</Aside>

## Closing a docket

Set the status in the app (`S`), swipe a row to Done, or run
`wh set D-12 --status done`. A docket whose intent has acceptance boxes closes
only on evidence: its required checks passed at one commit, its PR merged, its
Linear issue is completed, or its review is on GitHub. Without any, Wheelhouse
refuses and says why.

You can still close it on your word, with a reason. The record keeps the reason,
and the docket shows it closed on your word:

```sh
wh set D-12 --status done --anyway --reason "Covered by the wiki page"
```

Dockets without acceptance boxes close as before. See [Verify](/features/verify/).

## Holding a docket

`wh hold D-12 "Waiting on the DBA"` stops Build and Review from taking any step
on it until you resume it with `wh hold D-12 --off`. The reason is on the
record and on the docket.

## Finding dockets

The app's lists are views of the same dockets:

- **Inbox:** what needs you, from every agent.
- **Active:** what's in flight, grouped by what needs you (or by status, or by
  session). As a list, or as a board you drag cards across.
- **All** and **Done:** everything, and what's closed.
- **Agents:** every pane, in Rex and Herdr, under its docket.

From a terminal, `wh` shows what needs you and what's in flight,
`wh ls --all` lists every docket, and `wh show D-12` prints one docket's record.
