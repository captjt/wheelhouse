Wheelhouse is where you run all your agent work on your Mac. Each piece of work
is a **docket**. Your agents (Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity or Pi) run in
[Rex](https://www.superlogical.com/rex) or [Herdr](https://herdr.dev), side by
side. Wheelhouse follows them, briefs them and watches them. It asks you only
what you must decide, and keeps a record you can check.

## Who it's for

One developer running many agents in Rex or Herdr, across their own Macs. Your
work is in GitHub repos, with issues in Linear or GitHub. You review other
people's PRs and write your own, and you start agents by hand all day. You want
to steer all of it from one place, on the Mac or on your phone, without
watching panes.

With [Teams](/teams/overview/), several of those developers share the dockets
they choose, and their agents work together across Macs.

## What you get

- **A docket for every agent.** Every agent in a Rex pane or a Herdr terminal
  gets one: when it worked and when it waited on you, the files it made, and how
  it ended. For Claude Code, Antigravity and Pi, also what you asked and what
  the agent answered.
- **One Inbox.** Permission prompts, questions with answers as buttons,
  failures, finished turns and reviews ready to post, from every agent, on the
  Mac, in the menu bar, in a notification or on your phone.
- **Chat from anywhere.** Talk to any agent from your phone. What you send while
  it works is queued and goes in when its turn ends.
- **Steer a docket from one box.** Under its Activity: a note, `@tests` or
  `@all` to instruct its agents, `/ask` to ask them, `/decide` to record a
  decision they'll all work from, `/task` to add another agent.
- **Review and Build.** PRs that request you get a multi-agent review you
  approve before it posts. A docket's intent becomes a PR you approve before it
  opens, and review rounds become fixes you approve before they push.
- **Checks the hub runs itself.** Done rests on evidence: your repo's checks,
  run by the hub at the commit, recorded as witnessed.
- **Recap.** What happened today, this week or in 30 days, as a standup note,
  and the tokens it took.

## The ideas it rests on

1. **One record.** Every piece of work is a docket, and everything that
   happens to it is an entry. Every screen is a view of the record.
2. **Agents do, you decide.** Agents ask only what only you can decide. A
   question comes with options and a recommendation, and the agent keeps
   working on the recommendation while it waits.
3. **Nothing goes outward without you.** Review's and Build's agents don't
   push, post, or open a PR. Wheelhouse does those things for them after you
   approve, or under a standing order your config sets. Nothing ever merges.
   (An agent you start by hand in your own pane runs as you, with your logins:
   see [Security](/reference/security/).)
4. **Every claim says how it's known.** An entry is *said*, *witnessed* or
   *checkable*. A docket with acceptance boxes closes on evidence, not on what
   was said, unless you close it on your word, with a reason, on the record.
5. **The runtime holds the panes.** Panes live in Rex or Herdr. Wheelhouse
   never draws a terminal: **Open** takes you to the pane.
6. **Answer from anywhere.** The window, the menu bar, a notification, the
   phone, the keyboard, or a terminal.
7. **Quiet by default.** It notifies only for what needs you, and only once.

## The words it uses

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Docket** | One piece of work, from the first ask to the last merge, numbered `D-41`. It has a title, a status, an intent, and the issue or PR it came from. |
| **Intent** | A docket's living spec: what must be true when it's done, its acceptance boxes, and what's out of scope. |
| **Entry** | One signed, append-only record in a docket's log. A correction is a new entry that names the one it replaces. |
| **Protocol** | The playbook a docket follows: **Agent** (one you started by hand), **Review** or **Build**. |
| **Ask** | Something that waits on you: a permission prompt, a question, a gate, a failure, a message. Your Inbox is your open asks. |
| **Standing order** | A rule your config sets, shown as a sentence in Settings: when something happens, do something. "When a PR in acme/api requests me, Review it." |
| **Runtime** | The terminal multiplexer an agent's pane is in: Rex or Herdr. |
| **Hub** | `wh serve`: the process on your Mac that follows the runtimes, keeps the record and serves the apps. |

## How it fits together

The **hub** runs at login on your Mac. It follows Rex's and Herdr's events,
mirrors their sessions and panes, and gives each agent a docket. Claude Code's
and Antigravity's hooks, and Pi's extension, add your prompts and their turns.

The **record** is a local SQLite database: one signed, chained entry per thing
that happened. Keys are Ed25519, one per signer, on your Mac.

The **apps** are Wheelhouse.app on the Mac and the same app in a browser at
`http://127.0.0.1:7500`, or on your phone over Tailscale. Agents use the `wh`
CLI from their panes to read their brief, message you and each other, and keep
files on their docket.
