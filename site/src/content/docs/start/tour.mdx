You run agents all day: some you start by hand, some Review and Build start for
you. Wheelhouse keeps each on a docket and brings you only what needs you.
Here's a day.

## Morning, on the phone

There are three asks in the Inbox. You approve ENG-142's plan, answer an
agent's question with a tap on one of its options, and post the review of
api#341. The agents carry on. Nothing reached GitHub that you didn't approve.

## A PR requests your review

A docket opens for it. Three agents review it in a tab of their own, in Rex or
Herdr, without seeing each other's drafts. A checker merges what they found and
rates the merge risk. You get a notification for the checked review, with
**Post Review** and **Preview**. See [Review](/features/review/).

## You type `claude` in a pane

A docket appears, named by your prompt. You don't open it, and you don't need
to. When the agent finishes, its last message is on the docket as its turn. You
mark it done, or give it an intent and make it a Build.

<Shot name="mac-chat" alt="Chat with an agent: your prompt, its plan with a table, the files it kept, and a box to queue the next message while it works" />

## An agent hits a prompt

It wants to run the migration tests. You're in another app, so you approve it
from the notification, or the menu bar, or ⌥⌘K. Before a key goes in, the hub
reads the prompt again from the pane and checks it's still the one you saw.

## An agent needs a decision

It asks with `wh send --to me`: the question, its options as buttons with its
recommendation first, and what it's doing meanwhile. It keeps working on the
recommendation. You answer when you get to it, and the answer goes into its
pane between turns.

## You're away from your desk

You open the agent's docket on your phone and tell it to cover the 4xx case too.
It's mid-turn, so the message waits and goes in when the turn ends. Its reply
comes back as a push.

## Before you close a docket

The docket's intent has acceptance boxes. It closes on evidence: its repo's
checks, run by the hub at the commit, or its PR merged. An agent saying "tests
pass" isn't enough. See [Verify](/features/verify/).

## Friday

You open Recap for the week: reviews posted and their verdicts, PRs your agents
opened and merged, the decisions you made and what you chose, and the
tokens it all took, by provider and model. **Copy as Markdown** makes it a
standup note.
